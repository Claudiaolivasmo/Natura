About Natura Real Estate

A local team that blends experience in finance, tourism and real estate with a deep respect for the nature of La Fortuna and its surroundings.

Who we are

Natura Real Estate was born in La Fortuna de San Carlos with a clear purpose: to connect people with properties that reflect the true value of the area, its natural environment and its potential for both living and investing.

We work mainly in La Fortuna and Nosara, as well as in rural communities with strong growth potential. We know the area from the inside and understand what both local buyers and international investors are looking for.

Our team

Mauricio Olivas

Co-founder and lead agent at Natura Real Estate

With a solid background in finance, real estate and project management, Mauricio brings a strategic and transparent approach to every negotiation. His experience ensures that each client receives responsible advice, clear information and guidance based on real data from the market.

Claudia Olivas

Co-founder of Natura Real Estate

Claudia grew up in La Fortuna and has a strong background in tourism, hospitality, and information management. After living in Germany, she developed an international perspective that allows her to understand the expectations of foreign clients while staying deeply connected to the local market. Since 2019, she has been directly involved in vacation rental and property management, starting with the operation of her own property. In recent years, she has expanded this experience by working closely with homeowners, managing guest communication, coordinating operations, and ensuring high standards in every stay. Her approach combines a genuine connection to the territory, a hospitality-driven mindset, and meticulous attention to detail, helping position each property as a premium experience while maximizing its potential.

Our philosophy

At Natura we believe that buying or selling a property is much more than a transaction. It is a life decision and very often an important change for the people, families or projects we accompany.

This is why we work with strong ethics, personalised guidance and a deep understanding of the local market in La Fortuna and nearby areas. We take the time to listen to what you are truly looking for and to be honest about what each property can offer.

You can count on us to guide you with confidence and commitment at every step of the process.

How we work

  • Careful review of our inventory and selection of properties with real potential.
  • On-site visits, photography and detailed descriptions of each lot, home or project.
  • Clear communication regarding legal status, utilities, access and surroundings.
  • Close support throughout enquiries, visits and negotiations.
  • A long-term vision that favours responsible development in harmony with nature.
